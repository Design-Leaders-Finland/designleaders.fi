Design of Systems and Leaders

Helsinki, Finland

Patterns is a three-day retreat for 50 engineering, product, and design leaders who want to connect with and be inspired by peers and experts. Share challenges, develop relationships, and learn from the people whose work you admire in a shared safe space. Attendees will go home with the know-how to affect change and lead their teams towards achieving what used to feel impossible.

What is Patterns?

Patterns is a retreat at the gorgeous Bishop's Lodge in Santa Fe focused on leadership-level topics related to design systems. We're getting out of our day-to-day rhythms and journeying to a one-of-a-kind venue for an immersive three-day experience.

is patterns for you?

Patterns is for engineering, product, and design leaders—not frontline managers or makers. Attendees will be selected from a pool of applicants to create a community of peers from multiple industries and organizations. With only 50 spots, everyone at Patterns will be part of the conversation, and everyone's voice will be heard.

Frequently asked questions

Is Patterns a conference?

Patterns is intentionally designed as a retreat, not a conference, and provides an extended period of concentrated discussion, dialogue, and strategic thinking. Unlike a conference, attendees can expect to be fully engaged and involved in every aspect of the retreat.

Sounds fun but what will I take away from Patterns?

Patterns attendees will come away with a fresh perspective, new relationships, and actionable insights to make their design system efforts support other strategic imperatives, including:
‍

  • Create a high-performing team culture

  • Attract, onboard, and retain top talent

  • Ensure teams are shipping consistent, accessible, on-brand products and features across complex product ecosystems

  • Improve collaboration across design, engineering, and product orgs working in different locations and timezones

  • Innovate faster than competitors

  • Support company’s growth targets and metrics

When is it?

Patterns takes place on June 6-9, 2022 at Bishop’s Lodge in Santa Fe, New Mexico.

What should I bring?

The dress code is whatever makes you feel the most comfortable. June temperatures in Santa Fe average 83° during the day and 50° at night, so pack accordingly.

Are you able to accommodate guests with accessibility needs or dietary restrictions?

Yes. Once your application has been accepted, we will be in touch to confirm any accessibility needs or dietary restrictions.

What’s not included in the registration fee?

  • Air travel

  • Items for purchase on property and in your guest room

  • Food and beverage outside of the group agenda and meals offered within the Patterns experience

  • Spa services (though we have negotiated a discounted rate with Bishop’s Lodge)

Can I take work meetings during the trip?

We will have some longer periods of free time to use at your discretion. If, however, you aren’t able to attend all the scheduled sessions and activities, we encourage you to leave that spot for someone who is invested in attending the full retreat.

How are you handling COVID/Duty of Care?

Patterns is committed to creating a safe environment with attendees’ health as a priority. Patterns will require that all attendees arrive and present one of the following: proof of vaccination (CDC definition of fully vaccinated) OR proof of a negative COVID-19 test taken within 72 hours of main arrival day. Approved tests include molecular, PCR, and home testing kits.
‍
Patterns will follow the latest guidelines and mandates for health safety measures from the World Health Organization, Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, New Mexico and Santa Fe County. Patterns will follow applicable federal, state, and local laws, adapting event plans accordingly and sharing updates with you if a change in protocols is advised. We are also working closely with the Santa Fe community and Bishop’s Lodge and Auberge Resorts Collections and if they adjust their plans, we will communicate that as well. You can review current health and service practices of the resort here.

What is the cancellation and refund policy?

Cancellations made 30 days or more in advance of the event start date (Monday, June 6, 2022), will receive a 100% refund. Cancellations made within 30 - 14 days will incur a 25% non-refundable fee (based on registration ticket amount). Cancellations made within 14 - 3 days prior to the event will incur a non-refundable 75% fee (based on registration ticket amount). Cancellations made within 72 hours will not be refunded. Cancellations must be requested in writing by contacting patterns@knapsack.cloud. 

Tickets are non-transferable without an approved application for the new attendee. Application must be completed and approved in order to honor a transfer to another attendee. Transfers can be requested in writing by contacting patterns@knapsack.cloud. Cancellation policy will apply if application is not approved.

Can I invite other leaders from my company?

If you'd like to bring 1-2 additional peers from your company, we are allowing this on a case-by-case basis and providing a discounted group rate. Reach out to patterns@knapsack.cloud for details.

Did we miss something?

Reach out to patterns@knapsack.cloud for any other questions or concerns. We’d love to hear from you!
